# Brand & Style

The design system manifests an institutional yet forward-looking aesthetic tailored for a multinational infrastructure, venture, and technology collective. It synthesizes the gravitas of multi-sector corporate governance with the agility of advanced deep-tech exploration. The visual tone is characterized by calculated elegance, sovereign calm, and high-precision technical craft.

Drawing from modern technical brutalism wrapped in high-fluidity glass and organic pill surfaces, the interface evokes the feeling of descending into a deep-sea oceanic control bridge: profound, calm, luminous, and absolute. Interfaces should inspire unflinching trust among enterprise investors, regulatory bodies, and technical engineering leads. Surfaces emphasize smooth, continuous radii juxtaposed against rigid, monospaced-adjacent geometric titling.

## Colors

The color architecture anchors on deep-sea and abyssal layers. Surfaces transition upward from obsidian midnight (`#0B131B`) through rich nautical slate (`#102A43`) to the focused intensity of pine-teal (`#0F6B5B`).

- **Primary (`#0F6B5B`)**: Serves as the primary operational hue. It commands primary action flows, critical focus rings, active navigation traces, and brand anchors.
- **Secondary (`#102A43`)**: Acts as the architectural foundation. Applied to structural headers, parent shell containment, and secondary grouping zones.
- **Tertiary (`#C9A227`)**: A measured warm ochre and gold reserved for high-value strategic indicators, executive summaries, tier distinctions, and selective alert badges.
- **Neutral (`#0B131B`)**: Defines the true substrate. Surface elevation tiers are generated through successive teal-slate tint overlays (`rgba(15, 107, 91, 0.08)` to `rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.45)`).
- **Foreground / Text Tiers**: Purest text operates at `#F8FAFC`, secondary documentation and parameter values sit at `#94A3B8`, and disabled/structural boundary states resolve to `#475569`.

## Typography

The system enforces a dual-typeface typographic rhythm. `Space Grotesk` governs all display headings, subsidiary titles, labels, indices, and metric readouts, injecting structural futurism and engineering accuracy. `Plus Jakarta Sans` conducts all communicative prose, systemic descriptions, and dense enterprise data grids, delivering optimal optical comfort and clarity.

Large headlines employ tight negative tracking to compress spatial footprints and create an authoritative visual anchor. Micro labels and technical badges leverage extended letter-spacing and uppercase formatting to maximize scannability against dark surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid 12-column responsive grid underpins all digital viewports, re-stacking to 6 columns on tablet devices (768px - 1024px) and 4 columns on mobile viewports (< 768px). Outside borders maintain a generous margin tier to preserve executive breathing room, even at ultra-dense subsidiary portfolio resolutions.

Vertical rhythms align strictly to an 8px modular baseline. Component internal densities prioritize spacious interior padding (`space-md` through `space-lg`) paired with compact structural gaps, allowing cards and panels to read as monolithic, floating slabs rather than lightweight wireframes.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is realized through luminosity grading, specular teal rim lighting, and ambient refraction rather than harsh multi-tier cast shadows.

- **Base Layer (Elevation 0)**: Deep Obsidian ground (`#0B131B`) with an ultra-subtle radial wash of deep navy `#102A43`.
- **Card Slabs (Elevation 1)**: Translucent oceanic surface (`#102A43` at 40% opacity) treated with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and framed by a 1px border of `rgba(15, 107, 91, 0.25)`.
- **Raised & Active Panels (Elevation 2)**: Elevated slate container (`rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.85)`) complemented by a diffused emerald-teal drop glow: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(15, 107, 91, 0.35)`.
- **Overlays, Drawers & Modals (Elevation 3)**: High-density obsidian slate (`#0B131B` at 92%) rimmed with active gold/teal borders, casting an omnidirectional diffused ambient shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

In alignment with roundedness level 3, the design system employs sweeping, pill-shaped fluid contours for operational components, contrasted against structured container ratios.

- Standard buttons, search fields, category chips, and inline action items use full pill geometry (`9999px` / `rounded-full`).
- Structural modular cards, presentation showcases, and portfolio cards leverage sweeping corner curves (`2rem` / `rounded-lg`).
- Major presentation heroes, structural dashboard wrappers, and high-level dialogue modals scale to `3rem` (`rounded-xl`).
- Micro-elements such as indicator dots, active progress nodes, and numeric counters resolve to absolute spheres or fluid pill hulls.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary Action**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid pine-teal fill (`#0F6B5B`), text set in Space Grotesk (`label-md`, `#F8FAFC`). Subtle cyan inner specular highlight on hover, paired with an ambient teal glow (`0 0 20px rgba(15, 107, 91, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary Action**: Translucent dark navy backdrop (`rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.6)`), 1px border in teal stroke (`rgba(15, 107, 91, 0.4)`), smooth transitions to saturated slate on focus.
- **Tertiary / Strategic**: Pill button carrying a hairline Warm Gold accent border (`#C9A227`) with gold-tinted hover state, used exclusively for investor and subsidiary executive actions.

### Subsidiary Showcase Cards

Cards are structured as deep-slate rounded monoliths (`rounded-lg` / 2rem radius). The upper header integrates a subsidiary division pill tag, followed by a bold Space Grotesk headline. The card perimeter is bounded by a low-contrast gradient border flowing from `#0F6B5B` to `transparent`. Hover transitions trigger an upward translation of 4px and a teal border ignition.

### Input Fields & Controls

- **Form Inputs**: Continuous pill-shaped fields (`rounded-full`) with a dark translucent background (`rgba(11, 19, 27, 0.75)`), padded with `space-md` horizontal breathing room. Active focus removes standard outlines and applies a continuous pine-teal illumination ring (`#0F6B5B` at 100%, 2px outer spread).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Checkbox elements feature softened squircle envelopes (8px), while radio buttons utilize concentric glowing spheres. Active states glow with the pine-teal primary accent.

### Chips & Badges

Compact pill hulls (`rounded-full`) with uppercase Space Grotesk styling (`label-sm`). Subsidiary market status and financial indicators utilize deep muted backgrounds with high-contrast text and glowing tertiary (`#C9A227`) or primary (`#0F6B5B`) status dots.

### Lists & Metric Rows

Segmented data rows featuring semi-translucent hover backdrops with subtle corner rounding (`1rem`). Numerical data points and performance percentages render strictly in `Space Grotesk` tabular figures, maintaining pristine horizontal alignment.
